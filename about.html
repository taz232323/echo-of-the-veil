About the Author

J. A. Jacobs writes dark fantasy novels that explore the spaces between worlds—the thresholds where the living and the dead meet, where warnings echo through time, and where ordinary people discover extraordinary power.

Drawing on Irish mythology, urban fantasy, and the gothic tradition, Jacobs creates stories about messengers, threshold-keepers, and those who must choose between silence and the weight of knowing what others cannot hear.

The Echo of the Veil is the debut novel, introducing readers to a world where banshees are not monsters but messengers—and where the scream is not a curse, but a warning.
