About the Book

The scream is not a curse. It is a warning.

The site is structured for fantasy readers who need a fast entry point: what the story is, what kind of magic it uses, and where to buy or keep reading.

The Premise

In a city where the Veil between worlds is thin, some are born to hear what others cannot. When the screams begin, it is not just the dead who take notice.

Modern Banshees

Banshees are framed as messengers and threshold-keepers, not monsters. Their power is warning, memory, grief, and choice.

Read an Excerpt

Chapter One

The first scream came through the walls.

It was late, too late, and the city had already learned how to be quiet. Rain sealed the windows. My phone screen glowed on the counter, the only sound in the apartment a low, trembling hum.

Then it came again. A thin, tearing sound. Distant. Wrong.
